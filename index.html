A Brief History of Queer Culture

pada tahun 1960-an.

Queer history dates back to ancient civilizations, where diverse identities and relationships existed in societies such as Greece and Rome. Over time, queer people faced discrimination and restrictions. In the 20th century, they began fighting more openly for equality, acceptance, and the freedom to express themselves. Today, queer history celebrates diversity, courage, and the ongoing struggle for equal rights.

A Brief History of Feminism

pada tahun 1700-an

The history of feminism began in the 18th century, when women started fighting for equal rights. During the 19th and 20th centuries, the feminist movement grew to demand voting rights, education, and equal job opportunities. Today, feminism continues to fight for gender equality and against discrimination toward women.
